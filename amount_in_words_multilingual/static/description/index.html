Amount in Words – English & Arabic

Every quotation, sales order and customer invoice shows its total spelled out in the customer’s own language, on screen and on the PDF. Arabic follows real grammar: dual, plural and accusative forms, gender agreement and “فقط … لا غير”.

Why this module

In Saudi Arabia and the wider Gulf, the total in words is expected on commercial documents, and Arabic customers expect it in correct Arabic, not a word-for-word translation.

In standard Odoo 16

  • The standard quotation, order and invoice forms and PDFs have no total in words.
  • The currency method amount_to_text follows the logged-in user’s language, not the customer’s.
  • It appends one fixed unit label, so Arabic gets no dual, plural or accusative forms (ريالان / ريالات / ريالاً).
  • Numbers do not agree with feminine subunits such as هللة or بيسة.
  • Adding it yourself means custom QWeb and RTL styling on two report templates.
  • Hand-written text gets out of date as soon as a price, discount or tax changes.

With this module

  • Total (In Words) under the totals of every sales order and customer invoice, on screen and on the PDF.
  • Language taken from the customer’s profile, or forced to English or Arabic per company.
  • Every currency has singular, dual, plural and accusative Arabic wording, editable on the currency.
  • Full gender agreement: ثلاثة ريالات but ثلاث هللات, إحدى عشرة هللة.
  • Printed on the standard Quotation / Order and Invoices reports, right-aligned for Arabic.
  • Recomputed live from quantities, prices, discounts, taxes and cash rounding.

How it works

Install it, set your customers’ language and print. There is nothing else to configure.

1
Install
Currency wording is filled in automatically for 26 currencies. Existing orders and invoices show their words straight away.
2
Set the language
Set Language on the customer (English or Arabic), or choose Always English / Always Arabic in Settings.
3
Sell and invoice
The words appear under the totals and change as you edit lines, discounts, taxes or rounding.
4
Print
The quotation, order and invoice PDFs carry the same text, aligned left for English and right for Arabic.

Where to find it

No new menus: it lives where you already work

  • Sales › Orders › Quotations / Orders: Total (In Words) under the totals of each order.
  • Invoicing › Customers › Invoices / Credit Notes: the same line under the invoice totals.
  • Invoicing › Configuration › Accounting › Currencies: a new Amount in Words tab with the wording per currency.
  • Invoicing › Configuration › Settings › Amount in Words: language mode, “Only” wrapping and printing switches (the Accounting app on Enterprise).
Feature 1

Total in words on every sales order

Gulf Logistics Ltd has Language set to English, so order S00001 reads “Twenty-Six Thousand Two Hundred and Thirty-Seven Saudi Riyals and Twenty-Five Halalas Only”. Line discounts and the 15% VAT are already included: the words always match the Total.

Feature 2

Arabic for Arabic customers, whatever language you work in

The language comes from the customer, not the user. The same English-speaking salesperson opens order S00002 for مؤسسة النخبة التجارية and sees “فقط خمسون ألفاً وثمانمائة وستة وسبعون ريالاً سعودياً لا غير”. The order also has a Special Discount line of −1,000, which is reflected in the words.

For users working in Arabic, the label is translated (الإجمالي كتابةً) and the interface is right-to-left.

Feature 3

Updates live while you edit

The field depends on quantities, unit prices, Disc.%, taxes, subtotals, total-discount lines, the currency and the customer’s language. Here the discount on quotation S00004 is changed to 20% and, before saving, the words already read “One Thousand Six Hundred and Fifteen Euros and Seventy Cents Only”.

Feature 4

Customer invoices and credit notes, cash rounding included

Invoice INV/2026/00001, created from order S00002, carries the same Arabic text. Invoices read the grand total shown by the totals widget, so the words follow the form before saving as well.

With a Cash Rounding method, the rounded total is spelled out: 291.99 + 43.80 VAT = 335.79, rounded to 335.80, giving “فقط ثلاثمائة وخمسة وثلاثون ريالاً سعودياً وثمانون هللة لا غير”. Both the Add a rounding line and Modify tax amount strategies are supported.

Feature 5

Any currency, including 3-decimal ones

The document’s own currency is used, with the right number of decimals. Quotation S00003 is in Kuwaiti dinars: 697.150 becomes “فقط ستمائة وسبعة وتسعون ديناراً كويتياً ومائة وخمسون فلساً لا غير”.

Feature 7

Wording you control, per currency

The Amount in Words tab on each currency holds the English singular and plural, and the Arabic Singular, Dual, Plural and Accusative forms for the unit and the subunit, with an is Feminine switch for each. Empty fields fall back to the built-in vocabulary, and Reload defaults restores it. Currencies created later with a known code are filled in automatically.

Feature 8

Company settings

Under Invoicing › Configuration › Settings › Amount in Words: Amount in Words Language (Customer’s Language, Always English, Always Arabic), Wrap with “Only” / “فقط … لا غير”, and separate switches to print on quotations / orders and on customer invoices. All values are per company.

Arabic, done properly

The counted noun (التمييز) changes with the number. The module picks the form from the last two digits, exactly as a person would write it.

Worked example: SAR 1,234,567.89 → فقط مليون ومائتان وأربعة وثلاثون ألفاً وخمسمائة وسبعة وستون ريالاً سعودياً وتسع وثمانون هللة لا غير. Scale words follow the same rules (ألف / ألفان / ثلاثة آلاف / أحد عشر ألفاً), up to quadrillions.

Built for real-world corner cases

Each of these is covered by the code and, where it applies, by a test.

Existing documents
The field is computed, not stored: orders and invoices created before installation show their words immediately, with no migration.
Credit notes
The amount is spelled as a positive value (“Twenty-Three US Dollars Only”), as it appears on the printed credit note.
Journal entries and vendor bills
Miscellaneous entries get no words, and the invoice PDF block is limited to customer documents (invoices, credit notes, receipts).
Rounding without float noise
Amounts are rounded half-up with decimal arithmetic: 2.675 gives 68 subunits, not 67, and 99.999 becomes 100.
0- and 3-decimal currencies
Yen-style currencies drop the subunit; dinars and rials with 3 decimals count fils and baisa in thousandths.
Currencies without wording
Falls back to the currency’s Currency Unit and Currency Subunit labels instead of failing.
Other customer languages
French, Spanish, Urdu and others use Odoo’s standard amount_to_text in that language, with English as a safety net.
Customer without a language
The current user’s language is used, so the field is never empty.
Multi-company
Language mode, wrapping and printing switches are read from the document’s own company.
